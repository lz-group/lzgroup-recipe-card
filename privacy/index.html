Privacy Policy – Recipe Card & Grocery List

Publisher: Lamonz International Productions, LLC d/b/a LZ Group · Effective date: October 27, 2026

Short version: your recipes stay in your browser. The extension reads recipe content from the page you click it on and processes it only on your device. The extension doesn't collect, sell, or share personal data, and there are no ads or analytics.

What the extension reads

  • Only when you click the toolbar button, it reads the recipe data the site itself publishes for search engines (schema.org "Recipe" JSON-LD) on that one tab. It does not read anything else on the page, does not run in the background, and does not see your browsing history.

What is stored, and where

  • When you save a recipe, its text, photo link and the link to the recipe page are stored in your Recipe box. Saved recipes, your unit setting, your plan (Free/Pro) and your license key are stored locally with chrome.storage.local on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes this data. You can also delete any saved recipe from the Recipe box at any time.

Network requests

  • The extension makes no network requests of its own. There is no license server: your Pro key carries a digital signature that the extension checks on your device. No recipes, URLs, keys or browsing data are sent to us.
  • Recipe images: the card shows the recipe's photo, which your browser loads directly from the recipe's own website, the same as when you visit it.

Payments

  • Pro is a one-time $7.99 purchase sold through Link. Under Stripe Managed Payments, customers see Link as the merchant of record, and Stripe and Link handle the payment, receipts, payment questions and refund requests (how Managed Payments works). We never see or store your card number. Stripe's privacy policy covers the checkout: https://stripe.com/privacy.
  • After you pay, Stripe notifies our key delivery service so it can create your license key. That service stores only the checkout session ID, the license key issued for it and the key's license ID, so your confirmation page can show the key again. It doesn't store your name, email address or other buyer details.

Children

  • The extension is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone through the extension itself.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

  • The use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

  • We'll post changes here and update the effective date. Questions: support+recipe@lzgroupllc.com.
